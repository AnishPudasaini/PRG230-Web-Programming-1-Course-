Public Holidays in Nepal 2026

1. Maghe Sankranti – January 15, 2026

Maghe Sankranti is a major Nepalese festival celebrated on the first day of the month of Magh (mid-January), marking the end of the winter solstice and the transition of the Sun into the zodiac sign of Capricorn (Makar). In 2026, the festival is observed across Nepal on Thursday, January 15.
